Snapback

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Snapback helps you keep track of return windows and warranties. It is built so that your purchase information stays on your device. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

The short version

We do not run servers that store your purchases. We do not ask you to create an account. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your device

When you add a purchase, Snapback saves the item name, store, price, purchase date, return window, warranty length and, if you choose, a photo of the receipt. This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

If your device backs up app data (for example through iCloud or Google backup), your Snapback data may be included in that backup under the terms of Apple or Google. We do not have access to those backups.

Deleting a purchase in the app removes it and its receipt photo. Deleting the app removes all Snapback data from the device.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera or photo library access only when you choose to scan or pick a receipt. On iOS and Android you can limit access to selected photos. Images are used only to read the receipt and are never uploaded.

Receipt reading

Text on receipts is recognized on your device using Apple's Vision framework (iOS) or Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition (Android). The receipt image is not sent to us or to any other server for this purpose.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use push notification servers and do not receive information about your reminders.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipt or token from the store, and basic device information such as country and app version. It does not receive your purchases, receipt photos or any other content you add to Snapback. See revenuecat.com/privacy for details.

Children

Snapback is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because your purchase data is stored on your device, you can view, change or delete it at any time in the app. For questions about subscription data held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat, you may also contact us and we will help where we can.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email snapback.support@gmail.com.
